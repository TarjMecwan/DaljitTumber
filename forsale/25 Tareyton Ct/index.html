What's Special

Charming Ranch retreat with
arched gated entry & lush grounds.

This single-story home offers 1,979 square feet of comfortable living on a 7,000 square foot lot, with a distinctive arched entryway and iron gate leading to a private front courtyard.

Inside, vaulted ceilings and rich hardwood floors create an open, airy feel through the living and dining areas, which flow together beneath a wrought iron chandelier. The kitchen features white cabinetry, granite countertops, tile backsplash, stainless appliances, and a sliding door to the backyard.

The primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with soft natural light and an updated en-suite bathroom showcasing a curved glass shower, deep soaking tub, and tile flooring, while a secondary bedroom presents a bright window nook ideal for a child's room or office.

The fenced backyard includes a covered patio, storage shed, and a charming stone fountain tucked into mature landscaping. Located near top-rated San Ramon schools, this home is ready for its next owner. Schedule a private showing today.
